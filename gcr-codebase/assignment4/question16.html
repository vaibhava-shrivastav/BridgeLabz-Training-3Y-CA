URI Encoding Demonstration

Original URL with special characters:
https://example.com/search?query=HTML & CSS

Problem: The ampersand (&) and spaces in the query parameter need to be encoded.

Correctly Encoded URL:
https://example.com/search?query=HTML%20%26%20CSS

Explanation:

  • Space is encoded as %20
  • Ampersand (&) is encoded as %26
  • Other special characters follow similar patterns
