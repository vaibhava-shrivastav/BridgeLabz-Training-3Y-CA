The quiet power of a well-set page

Typography is not decoration placed on top of meaning. It is the pace, volume, and shape of the reading experience.

A serif face can feel measured, while a sans-serif face feels direct. Strong weight gives an anchor to a sentence, and highlighting one useful phrase can guide a scanning reader.

Good typography leaves enough space for thought. Adjusting line-height, letter-spacing, and alignment turns a crowded paragraph into a welcoming one.
